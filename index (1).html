Questions people ask first

What is Ascend360?

Ascend360 is a monthly health coaching program run out of North Palm Beach. You get one app for your meals and macros, your daily schedule, your supplements and any provider-prescribed protocol, plus a coach who checks in on video and adjusts the plan as you go.

Do I have to live in North Palm Beach?

No. Our office is at 751 Northlake Blvd, and many members are local to Palm Beach County, but coaching and consults happen by video, so you can join from anywhere we're able to serve.

How does the bloodwork work?

Practitioner consults include an at-home blood test kit from our lab partner, Rythm. You collect the sample at home, it's picked up, and your practitioner reviews the results with you. Monthly members retest around day 90 so you can compare.

Will I be prescribed peptides?

Only if a licensed provider decides it's appropriate after reviewing your history and labs. Many members do well on nutrition, sleep, training and supplement changes alone. Coaches never prescribe.

Can I keep my gym and trainer?

Yes. Training stays with your gym. Ascend360 handles the plan around it: food, recovery, sleep, supplements and labs. Several local gyms partner with us for exactly that reason.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Memberships bill monthly and you can cancel online at any time before your next billing date.

Does it work with my Apple Watch?

The Ascend360 app is built to sync with Apple Health and Apple Watch, along with other major wearables, so sleep, steps and heart-rate data flow into your weekly check-in.
