Privacy policy

Draft for legal review. Last updated [date].

This policy explains what Ascend Tips 360 ("we") collects through this website and how we use it. It covers this public website only. Health information you share inside the Ascend360 member platform is covered by a separate notice provided when you join.

What we collect here

  • Contact details you submit in our forms: name, phone, email, ZIP code, your interest and how you heard about us.
  • A referral code, if you arrived through a team member's link.
  • Basic website analytics, such as pages visited and device type.

Our forms don't ask for health information, and we ask you not to include it.

How we use it

To respond to your request, schedule calls and consults, credit referrals, and improve the website. With your consent, we may call or text you about your request. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (such as our website host and scheduling tools), and when required by law.

Your choices

Email info@ascendtips360.com to access, correct or delete the contact information you've given us.
