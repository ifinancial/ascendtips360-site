Pricing questions

Do I need a consult before joining?

Access can start anytime. Monthly and weekly coaching start after a consult, so your coach has your history and, for practitioner consults, your labs.

What's included with practitioner consults?

Consults with Lee and Diana include an at-home Rythm blood test kit and a results review.

Is the day 90 retest included?

Yes, for monthly and weekly coaching members. One-time consult clients can add a retest.

How do I cancel?

Online, from your account, any time before your next billing date. No phone call needed.

Are products included?

Membership covers coaching and the app. Any supplements or provider-prescribed products are billed separately, and you'll always see the cost before you order.
