How Ascend360 works

Five steps from first conversation to a plan that runs itself. Each step is paid before the next starts, so there are no surprise bills.

  1. Free 15-minute call

    Meet us at a gym, a market or an event, or book online. We talk through your goals and point you to the right first step. No cost, no pressure.

  2. Book your consult

    Choose a coach consult, a practitioner consult with Lee, or a VIP consult with Diana. Consults are booked and paid up front.

  3. At-home bloodwork

    Practitioner consults include an at-home blood test kit from Rythm. Collection takes a few minutes and the kit is picked up from your home.

  4. Results consult

    Your practitioner walks through your labs with you and sets your plan. If a prescription is appropriate, the licensed provider handles it directly.

  5. Go monthly

    Pick Access, monthly coaching or weekly coaching. Your plan, schedule and check-ins live in the app, and you retest around day 90.

What your coach does, and doesn't do

Coaches build and adjust your nutrition, training guidance, habits and supplement schedule, and they check in on video. They don't diagnose conditions or prescribe medication. Anything clinical goes to a licensed provider, and your coach sees the plan the provider sets so everything stays in sync.

What we need from you

Honest check-ins, a few minutes a day in the app, and your wearable if you have one. That's it.
