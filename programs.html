Programs

Every program is a 90-day track built on Bringing Homeostasis to the Terrain. You start from a proven plan and your coach adjusts it to you, so nothing is guesswork.

Weight loss

For people who've tried the diets and want something they can keep doing.

  • Daily nutrition plan with macros and easy swaps
  • Weekly lessons that build habits one at a time
  • Supplement and protocol schedule in the app
  • Weekly weigh-ins and waist tracking
  • Day 1 and day 90 labs
Ask about weight loss

Recovery

For people training hard, coming back from an injury, or dealing with joints that don't bounce back like they used to.

  • Recovery-focused nutrition and protein targets
  • Sleep and training-load tracking from your wearable
  • Mobility and recovery sessions alongside your gym program
  • Practitioner review of inflammation markers
  • Day 1 and day 90 labs
Ask about recovery

Sleep and stress

For people who are wired at night, tired all day, and running on caffeine.

  • Evening routine and caffeine timing plan
  • Sleep score and heart-rate variability tracking
  • Stress and focus check-ins each week
  • Herbal and supplement options reviewed by a practitioner
  • Day 1 and day 90 labs
Ask about sleep and stress

Energy and longevity

For people who want to feel sharp and strong for the long haul, and see it in their numbers.

  • Hormone, metabolic and nutrient markers reviewed
  • Nutrition built around healthy aging
  • Strength and recovery guidance to pair with your gym
  • Practitioner-led protocol when appropriate
  • Day 1 and day 90 labs
Ask about energy and longevity
